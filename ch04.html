ch. 4 — all good fun
from the summer i smoked the cursed weed

I left the shop fuming.
I walked fast, in the wrong direction of my house.
My brain was wracked in confusion. Too many thoughts.

Am I gonna get fired?
Where the hell did they go?
I have a crush on a dog person alien thing.
Am I a furry?
What parts does Perseid even have?
Is it fucked up to ask them that?

I texted Trevan.

where did you go?
did u leave already??

No response.
I withdrew into myself.
Something stirred in the corner of my eye.
I caught my reflection in the plate glass window of a coffee shop.
Black pants full of holes and a tattered white t-shirt, forever stained with barbeque sauce.
I walked faster, as if to outrun my shitty work clothes.

In my mind, I had already planned the perfect life for us.
Hanging out, smoking weed.
Throwing balls around.
Eating big meals filled with meat, no veggies.
Petting each other.
I was getting flustered. My face felt hot.
What is the matter with me? I wondered.
I didn't even notice that I had wandered back to Raven Park. It was dusk.
Might as well smoke some more, I thought.
I pulled the Spicy Jade out of my bag and rolled up another fattie.
Weed in, smoke out. I checked my phone.
One message from Trevan.

lol

FUCK YOU I wrote.
Instead of hitting send, I deleted the text and tucked my phone back in my pocket.
I looked around, desperate for anything to take my mind off of what they were doing.
The park was empty except for a busker, packing it in for the night.

He looked familiar to me.
The man was shabby.
Terrible half finished tattoos crept up his arms, and his hair was a mess of spikes.
His stomach was just visible from under his t-shirt.
"Rooster?"
The punk looked up. "Sorry bud, I'm done for the day. No autographs."
His expression hardened as he caught my face.
"Noel, is that you?" He coughed wetly into the crook of his arm as he stashed his guitar away. A dusty acoustic, grime caked up in the notches of the fretboard.
"Rooster, what are you doing out here? I haven't seen you in years."

He looked different. His face and body were softer and rounder then they were back then.
Old acne scars pockmarked his face. It reminded me of the moon.
"I'm on my forever tour, broski."
We always used to talk about that.
One final tour at the end of the world. Just driving until the wheels fell off.

We talked about doing that together.
The three of us.

"I can't believe you would continue Rat Juice without the rest of the band." I said wryly.
Rooster laughed, really loud.
His breath smelled like pure ass.
"It's just my self titled stuff now. Rooster!"
His hands gestured with pizazz, punctuating each syllable of his name.

He slung his guitar bag over his shoulder.
"How have you been." he snorted. "Still fucking shit up?"
I didn't know if he meant it in a good way or a bad way.
"Yup." I shrugged.
"How's Blanco Steve?" I said awkwardly. "Still playing bass?"

Rooster's face turned dark.
"Not sure. Last I heard he sold his bass to buy blow. I think he lives at home with his mom.
Can't get a job these days with an arrest on your record."
He shot me an accusatory glare.
I felt my pits and hands become drenched in sweat.
The last thing I wanted to do was start a fight.
What are the odds I would run into this fucking guy? I thought.
Today of all days.

I was emotionally tight, like a drum.
Maybe that's why the truth began spilling out of me like a cup overfilled.
Or a dam bursting.

"Listen...the reason I hid back then was because I had drugs on me."
I put out the joint against my shoe.
"Back then I was selling for my dad. He pressured me into it when I was real young. Like a kid. Pills, coke, stuff like that."
Rooster put his hands in his pockets and studied my face.
I couldn't make eye contact.
I wanted to bury myself alive.
Anything but talk about this.
"I don't do that anymore." I said finally.

Rooster's face softened.

"Hey man, you did what you had to do. I still think it was a bitch move, but it's not like things were easy for you either,
with your pops and all. How's he doing?"
I pondered that.
"Not well."
We stood together for a while saying nothing.
"Wanna get loaded?"
Rooster reached into the front pocket of his gig bag and produced a flask.
It was bound in leather, with a red chicken embossed on either side.
"Whiskey. I know that was your favorite back in the day."
"Yessssss." The small voice hissed in my mind.
It's not like I had anything better to do.

We walked off, down the main park path. The sun had set, and it had started to rain.
Rooster held his guitar bag over his spikes.
"Dawg, I know a spot where we can drink this shit." We headed towards the creek, uncorking the flask
once we were under the bridge that crossed it.
I took a long pull from the flask, no chaser.
"Yo broh, take it easy!" Rooster snatched the flask from me.
Whisky dribbled down my shirt. "Save some for me, dammit."

We shared a forlorn grin.
Chugging shitty whiskey in the rain seemed like something a younger us would do.
We talked for a long time.
Telling half remembered stories.

Blanco Steve's shitty gun that never worked and was only for show.
Spray painting the horses at the racetrack and losing security in the flea market next door.
Rooster's infinite nickel bag scheme.
Our post-hardcore band that never took off.
Time was running away from us, I thought.

After the last twenty-four hours I really wanted to hold on to that feeling of nostalgia and happiness.
Even if it was for just one moment, I wasn't thinking of Perseid and Trevan together.
I was fucked up. For this one millisecond, I was alive.

"Rooster," I said clumsily. "Can't we go back to being kids again?"
I took another swig out of the chicken flask.
"I'm sorry I didn't have your back when those stupid ass cops arrested you back then."
He just laughed.
"Nah I think it's too late for us man. We aren't fifteen anymore. We're in our mid twenties."
He took another swig of whiskey.
"That's basically ninety in punk years."
The rain stopped a while ago, and it was getting lighter outside.
"All that shit we did back then was all in good fun. So what if some of us got hurt along the way?" he trailed off.
I wasn't listening.
I was distracted by something happening near the edge of the creek.

Squawking and fluttering.
A bunch of sparrows were flocking around something writhing around in the mud.
I walked toward it to get a closer look.
It looked like an injured bird, another sparrow.
They were devouring it.
Each bird was taking tiny bites. Dark blood ran off into the creek, becoming pink and disappearing into the crystal waters.
One of the sparrows separated the injured sparrow's head from its body, ending it's struggle.
Sections of bone and spinal cord hung from its decapitated head.
The birds turned to face me, and their eyes were blood red.
I felt the booze in my stomach rise up and splatter in the dirt. I heaved again, spilling out even more of myself.

When I finally stopped puking my guts out, the birds were gone.
"Did you see that Rooster?" I turned around to see if he saw that shit.
I was alone under the bridge.
Did I fall asleep? I thought as I wiped the bile from the corners of my mouth.
Was that all just a dream? Or an omen?
The sun was high in the sky, and I knew in my heart that I was late again.
I opened up my phone.
My shift started at 11:00am.
The clock read 10:55.
I could still make it.
My shoes, legs, and ass were muddy as hell, and I was wearing the same barbeque stained shirt I wore
the day before.

I emerged from my spot under the bridge beside the creek.
As I hurriedly rushed out of Raven Park, an early morning kite flyer shot me an incredulous look and clutched his wallet.
I flipped him off.
